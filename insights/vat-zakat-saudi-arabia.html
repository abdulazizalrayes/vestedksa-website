Finance and tax

VAT and Zakat in Saudi Arabia

Tax readiness should be designed before the first invoice, employee, or supplier payment. Saudi VAT, Zakat, and income-tax workflows affect pricing, contracts, accounting systems, and management reporting from day one.

Published June 6, 2026 by Vested KSA

VAT registration

ZATCA states that taxable persons whose annual taxable supplies exceed SAR 375,000 are legally required to register for VAT, while annual taxable supplies above SAR 187,500 can make voluntary registration available. Establishments that are subject to VAT register through ZATCA and receive a dedicated VAT account number once registration is complete.

Zakat registration

ZATCA's Zakat journey explains that a Taxpayer Identification Number is generated after establishment registration with the Ministry of Commerce, after which the zakat-payer logs into ZATCA's portal to complete initial registration. Companies should not wait until year-end to understand who owns declarations, certificates, payment workflows, and records.

Operating reality: VAT and Zakat are not just finance topics. They affect customer registration, invoice formatting, procurement portals, supplier onboarding, and bank controls.

What international companies should set up

  • Tax owner and adviser responsibilities.
  • VAT registration decision and threshold monitoring.
  • Chart of accounts that can support local reporting and group consolidation.
  • Invoice format, Arabic requirements, VAT number display, and customer requirements.
  • ZATCA portal access, declaration calendar, certificate workflow, and payment approval controls.
  • Transfer-pricing, withholding-tax, and related-party workflow review where applicable.

Questions to answer before launch

  1. Will the Saudi entity issue invoices immediately, or is the first phase non-revenue?
  2. Who will review VAT treatment on services, imports, recharges, and intercompany flows?
  3. What reporting does headquarters need monthly?
  4. Which customer portals require VAT certificate, Zakat certificate, CR, or bank letters?
  5. How will approvals and payment records be stored for audit?

Vested KSA view

The strongest finance setup is boring in the best way: one tax owner, one clean filing calendar, one source of truth for invoices and supporting documents, and payment controls that headquarters can review without chasing local context.
