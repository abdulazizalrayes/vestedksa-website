Vested KSA - 90-Day Saudi Launch Plan

Use this planning sheet before committing budget, signing vendors, or hiring locally.

Phase 1: Days 1-30 - Entry Design

- Confirm the Saudi activity, ownership model, and likely regulatory path.
- Identify MISA, Ministry of Commerce, sector regulator, tax, HR, and banking dependencies.
- Decide whether the first phase needs an entity, branch, representative model, or managed operating presence.
- Build a decision log for headquarters: assumptions, approvals, risk owners, and open questions.
- Prepare parent-company documents, shareholder information, authorized signatories, and notarization/legalization needs.
- Map the first customer, supplier, tender, or hiring requirement that will drive setup urgency.

Phase 2: Days 31-60 - Formation and Operating Readiness

- Move formation, licensing, national address, Chamber, tax, social insurance, labor file, and bank-readiness tasks in one coordinated tracker.
- Define who owns weekly government follow-ups, document control, translations, and signature routing.
- Prepare accounting, invoicing, payroll, HR, supplier, and approval workflows before the first transaction.
- Confirm workspace, local address, IT, insurance, and vendor requirements.
- Create the first Saudi supplier/customer evidence pack: CR, tax/VAT records, bank letter, profile, policies, and authorized contacts.

Phase 3: Days 61-90 - Controlled Launch

- Move from setup tasks to operating cadence: weekly reporting, risk log, document archive, payment approvals, and management review.
- Onboard first employees, vendors, customers, and portals with clear owners.
- Confirm VAT/Zakat/e-invoicing readiness where applicable.
- Test invoice, payroll, expense, and procurement workflows before scaling spend.
- Review gaps with headquarters and agree the next 90-day scale plan.

Vested KSA Operating Principle

The goal is not only to register a company. The goal is to make the Saudi presence usable, auditable, and commercially ready.
