Vested KSA - Saudi Vendor Registration Evidence Pack

Use this checklist before applying to Saudi customer, supplier, tender, or procurement portals.

Company Identity

- Commercial Registration or foreign parent-company registration evidence.
- Legal name in English and Arabic, if applicable.
- Ownership chart and authorized signatory details.
- National address, office address, and primary Saudi contact.
- Chamber, MISA, municipal, sector, or other license details where applicable.

Tax and Finance

- Tax Identification Number.
- VAT registration certificate or VAT status explanation.
- Zakat/tax certificate workflow and owner.
- Bank account details and bank confirmation letter, when available.
- Invoice template and purchase-order handling process.

Compliance and Governance

- Anti-bribery and anti-corruption policy.
- Conflict-of-interest policy.
- Data protection and confidentiality controls.
- Health, safety, quality, and ESG policies where required by the buyer.
- Insurance certificates, if relevant.

Capability and Commercial Materials

- Company profile and short capability statement.
- Service/product categories mapped to the buyer's procurement taxonomy.
- Project references or anonymized experience summaries.
- Local delivery model and Saudi operating contacts.
- Arabic translation of key profile material if needed.

Portal Operations

- Portal owner and backup owner.
- Username, renewal, certificate-expiry, and document-update calendar.
- Record of submitted versions and buyer feedback.
- Follow-up cadence and escalation path.

Vested KSA Operating Principle

Vendor registration is market-access infrastructure. It does not guarantee work, but it prevents avoidable delays when an RFI, RFQ, tender, or customer onboarding request appears.
