## Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 23, 2026
### Introduction
Vested KSA ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website **https://vestedksa.com** (the "Site").
Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Site.
### 1. Information We Collect
#### Contact Form Information
When you fill out our contact form, we collect:
- Your name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Company name
- Message content
- Any additional information you choose to provide
#### Automatically Collected Information
When you visit our Site, we automatically collect certain information about your device and interaction:
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Pages visited and time spent
- Referral source
- Device identifiers
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies
### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
- **To respond to inquiries:** We use contact form data to reply to your messages and provide information about our services
- **To improve our Site:** Analytics data helps us understand user behavior and optimize the website experience
- **To send communications:** We may send emails related to your inquiry, service updates, or newsletters (with your consent)
- **To comply with legal obligations:** We process data as required by applicable laws and regulations
- **To protect security:** We monitor for fraudulent activity and ensure Site security
- **Business development:** To identify potential opportunities and market insights
### 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)
If you are located in the European Union or other jurisdictions with GDPR-like privacy laws, we process your personal data based on:
- **Consent:** You have explicitly consented to the processing
- **Contract:** Processing is necessary to fulfill a contract with you
- **Legal obligation:** We are required by law to process the data
- **Legitimate interests:** We have legitimate business interests in processing the data
### 4. Data Protection and Security
We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:
- SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
- Secure server infrastructure
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security audits and updates
- Confidentiality agreements with staff and contractors
However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
### 5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. These include:
- **Essential cookies:** Required for Site functionality
- **Analytics cookies:** Help us understand how you use the Site (Google Analytics)
- **Preference cookies:** Remember your settings and preferences
You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling certain cookies may affect Site functionality.
### 6. Third-Party Services
We use third-party services that may collect information about you:
#### Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics to analyze Site traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and reports website trends. You can opt-out of Google Analytics by installing the [Google Analytics opt-out browser extension](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) .
#### Email Service Providers
We may use third-party email services to send communications. Your email address may be processed by these providers in accordance with their privacy policies.
We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties before providing information or interacting with their services.
### 7. Data Retention
We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Typically:
- Contact form data: Up to 2 years
- Analytics data: 26 months (default Google Analytics retention)
- Email communications: Until you unsubscribe or 2 years, whichever is longer
- Transactional records: As required by applicable laws (typically 3-7 years)
### 8. Your Privacy Rights
Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:
#### GDPR Rights (EU Residents)
- **Right to access:** You can request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to rectification:** You can correct inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure:** You can request deletion of your data (under certain conditions)
- **Right to restrict processing:** You can limit how we use your data
- **Right to data portability:** You can receive your data in a portable format
- **Right to object:** You can object to certain processing
- **Right to withdraw consent:** You can withdraw consent at any time
#### Other Jurisdictions
If you are located in other jurisdictions with privacy laws, you may have similar rights. We will comply with applicable local laws.
### 9. International Data Transfers
Vested KSA is based in Saudi Arabia. Your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in Saudi Arabia and other countries. By using our Site, you consent to such transfers. We implement appropriate safeguards for international data transfers as required by applicable laws.
### 10. Children's Privacy
Our Site is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us personal information, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account immediately. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.
### 11. Links to Third-Party Websites
Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. This Privacy Policy applies only to our Site. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites before providing your information.
### 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our Site following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
### 13. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your privacy rights, or want to report a privacy concern, please contact us:
**Vested KSA** Email: [hello@vestedksa.com](mailto:hello@vestedksa.com) Location: Tahliyah St, Olaya Riyadh, Saudi Arabia Website: [https://vestedksa.com](https://vestedksa.com/)
We will respond to your request within 30 days or as required by applicable law.
### 14. Your Consent
By using our Site and providing us with personal information, you consent to the collection, use, and processing of your data as described in this Privacy Policy.
