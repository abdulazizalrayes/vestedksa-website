## Regional Headquarters (RHQ) Program in Saudi Arabia
Saudi Arabia's RHQ program is relevant for multinational companies that want the Kingdom to become a regional management base, not just a sales market. The decision should be made around substance, governance, and operating capability.
Published June 6, 2026 by Vested KSA
### What the RHQ program is
MISA describes RHQ as a program inviting global companies to relocate regional headquarters to Saudi Arabia, established as a joint initiative between the Ministry of Investment and the Royal Commission for Riyadh City. MISA's FAQ describes the program as a joint initiative to position the Kingdom as a leading regional hub for multinational companies.
### Who should evaluate RHQ
- Multinational companies managing a Middle East or broader regional portfolio.
- Companies seeking a Saudi-based regional leadership, strategy, finance, HR, procurement, or shared-services center.
- Businesses that expect government, giga-project, or strategic-account work where Saudi presence and decision-making depth matter.
- Groups that can support real local activity, not just a mailbox or nominal address.
**Core question:** can the RHQ entity demonstrate real regional management activity, appropriate leadership, and clean governance records?
### What to prepare
1. Regional scope, countries covered, and functions to be managed from Saudi Arabia.
2. Leadership roles, decision rights, job descriptions, and reporting lines.
3. Corporate approvals and group structure documents.
4. Office, HR, payroll, tax, banking, and finance operating model.
5. Internal controls for intercompany services, approvals, expenses, and compliance records.
### Where RHQ projects go wrong
Common issues include treating RHQ as a branding exercise, underestimating local staffing and payroll setup, failing to define intercompany service flows, not preparing finance controls, or creating an entity that is not ready to support procurement and customer expectations.
### Vested KSA view
RHQ can be powerful when it matches a genuine regional operating mandate. Before applying, leadership should decide which activities will move to Saudi Arabia, who owns decisions locally, what evidence supports the RHQ substance, and how the entity will run day to day.
