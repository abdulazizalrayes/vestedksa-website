## Who we are.
A team built to help international companies succeed in Saudi Arabia.
### Our Story
When international companies decide to enter Saudi Arabia, they face a gap that no single consultant can fill. They need operational expertise, local market knowledge, compliance guidance, and a partner they can trust—someone who understands both the expectations of Western executives and the realities of Saudi business.
Vested was founded by people who lived this gap. We've spent years working across the border, navigating the complexity, building networks, and understanding what it truly takes to help a foreign company not just establish itself in Saudi Arabia, but grow there—compliantly and confidently.
We built Vested because we realized that the best operations partner needed to be someone accountable, verified, and transparent. Someone who would be there from day one through year five. Someone who would move at startup speed while respecting Saudi Arabia's market dynamics. That's us.
